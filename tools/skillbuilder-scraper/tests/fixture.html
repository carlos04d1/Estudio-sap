AWS Control Tower

Transcript

AWS Control Tower is a comprehensive solution for setting up and governing a secure, compliant, multi-account AWS environment.

Controls

Controls are categorized according to their behavior and their guidance.

  • Preventive – disallows actions that lead to policy violations.
  • Detective – detects noncompliance of resources.
  • Proactive – scans your resources before they are provisioned.
